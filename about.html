Journey & History

MILESTONE of MVNet

Growing to solution connectivity provider, MVnet continues to move forward with innovation, reliability, and a commitment to delivering better connectivity. Every milestone represents our journey in building trusted solutions, expanding our network, and empowering businesses and communities through seamless digital connectivity.

2017 – MVNET PRESENCE

MVnet was founded with the goal of enhancing digital connectivity.

2018 – LAUNCHED HIGH SPEED BROADBAND INTERNET

Successfully launched high-speed broadband internet services.

2019 – EXPANSION INITIATIVES

Expanding MVnet service to wholesale segment.

2020 – REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

MVnet completed the fulfillment of (Jartup) & (Jartaplok).

2021–2023 – STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP

  • Enhancing MVnet cable portfolio to serve digital connectivity.
  • Acquisition of Whole Datahall 3 and Half of Datahall 2.
  • Expand residential business operation in Palembang.

2024–2025 – COVERAGE EXPANSION

  • Expanding coverage in West Java, Central Java and East Java.
  • Operated first SubSea Cable from Batam to Singapore.
  • Expand to Bali island and opening of our first branch office.

2026 – TRANSFORMATION

  • Completed MVnet own NAP License.
  • Expanding our backbone cable to Lombok island.
